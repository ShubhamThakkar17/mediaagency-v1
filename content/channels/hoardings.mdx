## How it works in India

Hoardings are everywhere in India, from metro junctions to state highways. Sites are owned by hundreds of local media owners and licensed by municipal bodies, so availability and rules differ from city to city.

A site is rented by the month. The price depends on size, traffic, visibility, lighting and how sought-after the junction is. Printing and mounting are charged separately.

## When it earns its place

Outdoor is the fastest way to make a brand feel present in a place.

- Real estate launches that need to own the approach roads to a site.
- Retail, jewellery and showroom openings.
- Hospitals, schools and colleges building a name in their catchment.
- Highway corridors for auto, fuel, cement and hospitality brands.

It works around the clock and cannot be skipped, blocked or muted.

## How we plan and buy it

We plan routes, not just sites. Our team maps how your audience travels, then picks sites along those journeys for repeated exposure.

Every shortlisted site is checked for visibility distance, angle, obstructions and night lighting. We negotiate directly with site owners and bundle sites across cities to improve rates. Because we execute across states, one plan can cover a metro, its satellite towns and the highway between them.

After mounting, we monitor with dated photographs and replace damaged flex quickly.

## What to watch

A bad site is wasted money at any price. Check for trees, wires and competing clutter. Monsoon weather can damage flex and delay mounting. Municipal rules change, and unauthorised sites can be removed without notice, so we use licensed inventory only. Outdoor builds awareness, not clicks, so judge it with search lift, walk-ins and recall. All cost figures here are indicative planning ranges.
