## How it works in India

The railway network touches almost every district in India and carries crores of passengers. Media rights are managed zone by zone and division by division. Inventory includes train exteriors, coach interiors, station hoardings, foot overbridges, digital screens and audio announcements.

Media is rented by the month, by train or by station package. Long-distance trains pass through dozens of towns on a single run, which gives one wrap an unusually wide footprint.

## When it earns its place

- Brands building reach in tier 2, tier 3 and rural markets.
- FMCG, agri inputs, telecom, small loans, insurance and two-wheelers.
- Education brands recruiting students from smaller towns.
- Solar, cement and building material brands following a state corridor.

Railways are strongest in the North, East and Central zones, where trains are the main way to travel between towns.

## How we plan and buy it

We plan by corridor. Our team overlays your distribution map on train routes, then picks trains and stations that serve those districts.

We choose between on-train and at-station media by task. Wraps deliver range. Station media delivers repeated exposure to the same town. Creative is written in the language of the route and kept simple, with a strong visual, the brand and one benefit. We manage approvals with the railway divisions and document every installation.

## What to watch

Processes are slower and more formal than in commercial outdoor, so plan early. Trains get dusty and wraps fade, so maintenance and periodic checks matter. Premium and affluent audiences are better reached at airports. Measurement is directional rather than precise. The cost figures here are indicative planning ranges and vary by zone, train and station class.
