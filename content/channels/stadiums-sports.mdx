## How it works in India

Sport in India means cricket first, with kabaddi, football, badminton and running events growing behind it. Rights sit with boards, leagues, teams, venues and broadcasters, so a sports plan is usually a combination of deals.

Inventory includes perimeter boards, jersey positions, tournament partnerships, broadcast and streaming spots, in-stadium screens and fan zones. Pricing is per match, per season or per property.

## When it earns its place

- A brand that wants national fame quickly.
- Categories with broad appeal, such as FMCG, fintech, auto, two-wheelers and consumer tech.
- Launches timed to the big cricket season.
- Regional brands backing a home team to earn local loyalty.

Sport is watched live, in groups and with emotion. Brands that show up consistently become part of the ritual.

## How we plan and buy it

We decide the role first: reach, association or engagement. Our team then compares properties on audience, cost and clutter. A smaller league where you are the lead partner can beat a crowded major event.

We negotiate the full rights package, including digital content, tickets and hospitality. Then we build an activation plan across television, social, retail and outdoor, so every rupee of rights works harder.

## What to watch

Rights are only the entry fee. Budget for activation as well, or the association goes unnoticed. Clutter is heavy in the biggest events, where dozens of brands compete for the same eyes. Results depend partly on team performance and weather. Direct response is weak, so judge sport on fame and affinity. The costs shown are indicative planning ranges, and major properties sit far above them.
