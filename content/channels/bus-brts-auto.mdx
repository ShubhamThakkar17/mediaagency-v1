## How it works in India

Buses and autos are the daily transport of most Indian cities. City bus corporations, BRTS systems and state road transport fleets sell exterior and interior branding. Auto-rickshaws and e-rickshaws are branded through unions and local vendors, usually with hoods or back panels.

Pricing is per vehicle per month, plus printing and fitting. It is among the lowest-cost media per person reached.

## When it earns its place

- A brand that needs to be seen all over a city on a modest budget.
- Coaching institutes, schools and colleges in admission season.
- Clinics, local retail, food brands and mobile or loan offers.
- Tier 2 cities where hoardings are few but buses and autos are everywhere.

Inter-city state transport buses also carry a message from a city into the surrounding towns and villages.

## How we plan and buy it

We match vehicles to geography. Our team selects bus depots and routes that pass through your priority areas. For autos, we recruit from stands near markets, colleges and stations.

We keep designs simple and large, because both the vehicle and the viewer are moving. We handle permissions with the transport body, manage printing and fitting, and document every vehicle. Mid-campaign checks keep the fleet looking fresh.

## What to watch

Vehicles get dirty and branding gets damaged, so maintenance is part of the job. Routes can be reassigned by the transport authority. The audience is broad, which is efficient for mass products but wasteful for niche or premium ones. It builds familiarity rather than detailed understanding, so pair it with a channel that explains the offer. Costs shown are indicative planning ranges and vary by city and fleet.
