## How it works in India

India has millions of active creators across short video, long video and photo platforms. They work in every language and niche, from beauty and food to finance, farming and technology. Brands pay per deliverable, such as a video, a set of stories or a live session.

Creators are usually grouped by follower size: nano, micro, macro and celebrity. Pricing rises with reach, but engagement often falls as accounts get bigger.

## When it earns its place

- Launching a product that benefits from being shown and explained.
- Reaching Gen Z and young professionals who skip traditional ads.
- Building trust in a new D2C, fashion, beauty or food brand.
- Reaching regional audiences in their own language and humour.

A creator's recommendation feels like advice from someone familiar, which is why it moves consideration so well.

## How we plan and buy it

We select on audience, not follower count. Our team checks each creator's audience location, engagement quality and past brand work, and screens for fake followers.

We write a brief with the key message, the mandatories and the disclosure label, then leave the style to the creator. We secure usage rights so strong content can run as paid ads. Every creator gets a tracked link or code, and we report results creator by creator.

## What to watch

Over-scripted content performs poorly, so trust the creator's voice. Vet for brand safety and past controversies. Vanity numbers can mislead, so look at saves, shares and sales, not just views. One-off posts fade quickly, and repeated work with the same creators builds more trust. The costs shown are indicative planning ranges and vary widely by creator.
