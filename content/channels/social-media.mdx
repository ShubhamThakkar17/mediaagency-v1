## How it works in India

Hundreds of millions of Indians use social and short video apps daily, across metros and small towns, in every major language. Advertising is bought through platform auctions. You set a budget, an audience and an objective, and the system finds people likely to respond.

You pay by impressions, clicks or results. There are no fixed rate cards, and costs move with competition and season.

## When it earns its place

Social is the most flexible channel in the plan.

- Lead generation for real estate, education, healthcare and financial products.
- Sales for D2C, fashion and retail.
- Launch amplification alongside outdoor, print or television.
- Retargeting people who visited your site or watched your video.

It works at almost any budget, which makes it the natural first channel for a growing brand.

## How we plan and buy it

We structure campaigns by funnel stage. Video builds an audience. Engagement and traffic campaigns warm it up. Lead or sales campaigns convert it.

Our team produces many creative variations and lets the data pick winners. We write in the language of each market and refresh creatives every few weeks. Tracking is set up before launch, with the pixel, conversion events and CRM link in place. We review lead quality with your sales team weekly and shift budget accordingly.

## What to watch

Cheap leads are not always good leads, so judge on qualified leads and sales. Creative fatigue sets in quickly, and without fresh assets costs rise. Platforms change formats and targeting options often. Attribution is imperfect, and social often gets credit that outdoor or television helped earn. The cost ranges shown are indicative, since real costs are set by live auctions.
