## How it works in India

India has a deep culture of celebrity endorsement. Film stars, cricketers and regional icons front brands in every category, from jewellery to cement. An endorsement is a contract for a term, usually one to two years, covering a set number of shoot days, the media you may use and exclusivity within your category.

The fee buys the association. You still need media, on television, print, outdoor and digital, to put that face in front of people.

## When it earns its place

- A new or little-known brand that needs trust quickly.
- Entry into a new state, led by a face that state loves.
- Categories where reassurance matters, such as jewellery, finance, education and housing.
- A relaunch that needs to signal change.

In tier 2, tier 3 and rural markets, a known face on a hoarding or a shop board carries real weight with consumers and dealers.

## How we plan and buy it

We begin with fit, not fame. Our team shortlists personalities by audience match, regional pull, existing endorsements and reputation risk. We then negotiate terms and manage contracts through to the shoot.

We plan the content in one go: film, stills, regional language versions and social assets. That keeps shoot days productive. The media plan is built at the same time, so the association is visible from the first week.

## What to watch

A celebrity is not a strategy. Without a clear idea, people remember the star and forget the brand. Stars who endorse many brands deliver less for each. Reputation risk is real, so contracts need conduct and exit clauses. Advertising rules require endorsers to make honest claims, and we check scripts against them. Fees quoted here are indicative planning figures only.
