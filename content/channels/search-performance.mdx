## How it works in India

When Indians want a flat, a course, a doctor or a loan, they search for it. Paid search places your ad above the results for the terms you choose. You pay only when someone clicks.

Prices are set by auction. Competitive categories and metro locations cost more per click. Around search sit related formats: shopping listings, map listings, performance display, video action ads and remarketing.

## When it earns its place

Search belongs in almost every plan with a lead or sales goal.

- Real estate, education, healthcare, BFSI, auto and solar enquiries.
- E-commerce and D2C sales.
- Local services that people look for nearby.
- Catching the demand created by a hoarding, print or television burst.

It reaches people who have already decided to act, in every audience segment.

## How we plan and buy it

We build from intent. Our team groups keywords by how close the searcher is to buying and bids hardest on the terms that convert.

We write ads in English and in regional languages, and we target by city, pin code and radius. Negative keywords cut wasted clicks. We build or refine the landing page, set up call and form tracking, and connect the CRM. Budgets then move weekly towards the campaigns with the lowest cost per qualified lead.

## What to watch

Search cannot exceed existing demand. Once you capture most relevant searches, extra budget brings little. Poor landing pages and slow sales follow-up waste good leads. Brand terms look cheap but often capture people who would have come anyway, so report them separately. Volumes are lower in tier 3 and rural markets. All cost figures here are indicative planning ranges.
