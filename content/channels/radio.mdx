## How it works in India

FM radio is a city medium. Each station serves one city and its surrounding area, usually in the local language with a strong mix of film music. Listening peaks in the morning and evening drive times, in cars, cabs, buses, shops and on mobile phones.

Airtime is sold in 10 second units. Rates vary by city, station rank and time band. Beyond spots, stations sell RJ mentions, sponsorships and on-ground activity.

## When it earns its place

Radio is at its best when the message is local and time-bound.

- A store, clinic or showroom opening.
- A weekend sale, a festive offer or an exhibition.
- Admission deadlines and event dates.
- Sustained reminders for a retail chain in its home cities.

It is also a low-cost way to add frequency to a print or outdoor campaign.

## How we plan and buy it

We choose stations by the audience they deliver in your catchment, not by overall rank alone. A second-ranked station with the right listeners is often the better buy.

Our team plans enough spots a day to build frequency, then adds RJ mentions for warmth and credibility. We write for the ear: one idea, the brand name early and often, and a simple action such as a missed call. Scripts are adapted for each language instead of being translated word for word.

## What to watch

Radio has no visuals, so it cannot carry complex products on its own. Listeners are often doing something else, which is why repetition matters more than clever copy. Measurement is lighter than in digital channels, so build in a response mechanism. Coverage is thin in rural areas, where television and railways do the heavier work. The costs shown are indicative planning ranges and vary by city and season.
