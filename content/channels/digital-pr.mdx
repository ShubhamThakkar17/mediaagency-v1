## How it works in India

Indian readers get much of their news online, from national business sites to regional language portals. Digital PR places your story in those environments. Some coverage is earned through news value and media relationships. Some is paid for, as sponsored articles or native placements labelled as partner content.

Paid placements are priced per article or by impressions. Earned work is usually a monthly retainer.

## When it earns its place

- Categories where trust decides the sale, such as healthcare, BFSI, education and renewable energy.
- B2B companies selling to business owners and procurement teams.
- Funding announcements, leadership hires and new facilities.
- Brands whose search results need stronger, more recent stories.

Before a large purchase or partnership, people search for the company. What they find shapes the decision.

## How we plan and buy it

We start with the story. Our team finds angles that are useful to readers: data, expert comment, customer outcomes or a clear point of view.

We map the publications your audience reads, in English and regional languages. We pitch earned stories to journalists and book paid placements where control over timing and message matters. Articles are written to be found in search, and the best ones are amplified through social and native ads.

## What to watch

PR builds reputation slowly and does not replace lead generation. Earned coverage cannot be guaranteed or dictated. Sponsored content must be labelled, and readers notice when it reads like an advertisement. Weak stories do not travel, however much is spent. Mass consumer reach in tier 3 and rural markets is limited. The costs shown are indicative planning ranges.
