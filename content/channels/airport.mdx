## How it works in India

Air travel has grown fast, and India now has busy airports well beyond the metros. Each airport appoints a media concessionaire to sell its advertising. Inventory ranges from large backlit panels and video walls to baggage belts, aerobridges, trolleys and lounges.

Sites are rented by the month. Price follows passenger volume, the zone inside the terminal and the size of the site.

## When it earns its place

- Brands selling to business owners, senior executives and affluent families.
- Premium real estate, wealth management, cars, watches and jewellery.
- B2B companies that want to be seen by investors, partners and talent.
- Corporate brand campaigns that need stature more than volume.

Flyers arrive early and wait. Few other media give a premium audience this much unhurried time.

## How we plan and buy it

We map the passenger journey from kerb to gate. Our team picks sites where people pause, such as check-in queues, security lanes, boarding gates and baggage belts.

We match airports to your markets instead of defaulting to the largest hub. Creative is clean and premium, with a short line and strong branding. For cars and property, we explore display zones so people can see the product itself.

## What to watch

Airports are expensive per contact, so they suit high-value products only. Popular sites are booked for long tenures, which limits short-notice availability. Security rules slow down installation and changes. The audience is narrow by design, so do not expect mass reach, and tier 3 and rural buyers are barely present.
