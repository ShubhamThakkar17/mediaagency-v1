## How it works in India

Domestic air travel connects well over a hundred Indian cities. Airlines sell media on their own assets, either directly or through specialist agencies. Options include the in-flight magazine, seat-back panels, tray tables, overhead bins, boarding passes and, on some carriers, screens or streaming entertainment.

Packages are sold by aircraft, by route or by a share of the fleet for a month or longer.

## When it earns its place

- Brands that want senior professionals and business owners in a relaxed state.
- Premium property, financial services, hospitality and tourism boards.
- B2B brands building familiarity with decision-makers.
- Products that benefit from a longer read or a QR code scan.

A flyer spends one to three hours a few inches from your message. That is rare attention.

## How we plan and buy it

We start with the routes your buyers fly. Our team builds packages around those city pairs, then chooses the format by task. Tray tables and seat-backs give constant visibility. Magazines carry detail and long shelf life. Boarding passes are held and kept.

We write for a reader with time: a clear benefit, a reason to believe and a QR code that works offline until landing. We coordinate airline approvals and fitting schedules well ahead.

## What to watch

Reach is small in absolute terms, so this is a precision medium, not a volume one. Aircraft are rotated across routes, so targeting is good but not exact. Lead times are long and changes mid-campaign are difficult. It rarely justifies its cost for low-value, mass products. All cost figures here are indicative planning ranges.
