# Image prompts for empty media slots

These slots in `content/media.ts` have no stock asset yet. On the site they show the branded animated fallback from `<SmartMedia>`. Each prompt below can generate a replacement. When you add one, store it in `content/media.ts` with `licence: 'Generated'` (or `'Own'`), a factual `alt` and a `sourcePage`, then run `pnpm check:media`.

House style for every prompt: photoreal, Indian city context, blue hour or warm studio light, an orange #FF6036 accent on the media surface, ink and cream tones, 3:2, no real brand logos, no legible text.

The video slots get a poster-frame prompt: a single still that works as the `poster` image and as the first frame of a short muted loop.

## vid-city-traffic

Poster frame. Photoreal elevated view of a wide Indian city arterial road at blue hour, with a time-lapse feel: long red and white light trails from cars, buses and auto-rickshaws streaming under a flyover. Mid-rise buildings and shop signs are softly out of focus. One large blank roadside hoarding glows with a flat orange #FF6036 panel. Deep ink sky fading to cream at the horizon, 3:2, no real brand logos, no legible text, no identifiable faces.

Polished cream floor reflections, 3:2, no real brand or airline logos, no legible text.
